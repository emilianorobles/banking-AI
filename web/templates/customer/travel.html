File a travel notice
Two rules stop firing while a notice is active: GEO_FOREIGN and GEO_NEW_COUNTRY. Everything else — amount anomalies, velocity, card testing, impossible travel — keeps running exactly as before. A travel notice is not a blank cheque, and the drill-down on any transaction will show you which rules were suppressed and which still ran.

Plan a travel budget

What it uses: your average daily spend over the last 90 days, your typical transaction size, and the destination's cost level relative to home — then shows the arithmetic so you can argue with it.
