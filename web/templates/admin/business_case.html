Where the honest limits are
The 25% is not 25% wrong

The harness counts a challenge as a false positive against a ≤10% target, so it prints BELOW TARGET. Nothing legitimate is ever blocked — the 25% is customers asked to confirm. Step-up verification is the correct action on a genuinely ambiguous transaction, and the target is stricter than the behaviour deserves.

Retrieval needs the primary provider

Chat fails over to a second provider; embeddings do not, because the fallback serves no embedding model. Retrieval degrades to its cache, so citations survive for recorded scenarios but a brand-new transaction gets no precedents while the primary is down. Pre-flight says so in as many words.
