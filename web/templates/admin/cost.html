Be honest about this in the pitch

The rules are strong enough that the model adds little raw detection accuracy — recall is 100% either way. Its real value is the explanation an analyst needs to act, the cited precedent that makes the decision auditable, and the learning loop that turns each resolved case into future retrieval. Saying that, and showing you measured it, is worth more than claiming an improvement you cannot evidence. The A/B is on the Evaluation page.
